Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting medical treatment in India — wherever you live.

Safety & Quality

Is medical treatment in India safe for international patients?+

Yes. India has dozens of JCI-accredited hospitals — the same international body that accredits top US hospitals like Johns Hopkins and Cleveland Clinic. Our partner hospitals maintain infection control, surgical outcomes, and patient safety standards aligned with leading centres worldwide. We only work with accredited institutions that meet international benchmarks.

What are JCI and NABH accreditations?+

JCI (Joint Commission International) is the global gold standard for hospital accreditation, based in the USA. NABH (National Accreditation Board for Hospitals) is India's equivalent, with standards aligned to international benchmarks. Both require regular audits of clinical outcomes, patient safety, infection control, and facilities.

Are the doctors comparable to specialists in my home country?+

Many of our partner doctors trained at institutions like Mayo Clinic, Cleveland Clinic, Johns Hopkins, Harvard, Oxford, and other leading centres worldwide. They are published in international journals, members of global medical societies, and often perform higher volumes of specialised procedures than typical Western counterparts — which can translate to strong outcomes.

What happens if something goes wrong during or after treatment?+

All our partner hospitals have complication management protocols and international patient guarantee policies. After you return home, you typically have extended telemedicine follow-up access with your Indian specialist. If in-person care is needed locally, we help coordinate with clinicians where possible. We strongly recommend travel medical insurance that explicitly covers your procedure and any complications.

Costs & Insurance

How much money will I actually save?+

Savings typically range from 60–90% depending on the procedure and your home market. For example, a 3000-graft hair transplant often costs a small fraction in India compared with typical high-cost markets; dental implants and major surgery show similar gaps. Flights and accommodation are extra, but total cost usually remains far below care at home. Use our cost calculator for indicative comparisons.

Will my national or public health plan cover treatment in India?+

Most public schemes only cover care in your home jurisdiction; overseas elective treatment is usually not reimbursed. Some private insurers offer limited overseas or travel benefits — check your policy wording. We can provide itemised invoices and medical documentation in formats commonly used for international claims.

Are medical expenses overseas tax-deductible?+

Tax rules vary by country and personal situation. A qualified tax adviser in your jurisdiction can tell you whether overseas medical costs qualify for relief or credits. We provide itemised hospital bills, physician receipts, and medical reports to support any claim your adviser recommends.

Do I need travel insurance?+

We strongly recommend it. Standard travel insurance often excludes pre-planned elective procedures, so look for a medical travel or complication-specific product where available. Major global insurers and specialist brokers offer policies tailored to medical travel. We can discuss what to look for for your procedure — final choice should align with your insurer's terms.

What payment methods are accepted?+

Hospitals accept international credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), bank transfers, and cash. Most patients pay a deposit (20-30%) before travel and the balance at the hospital. We can help arrange payment plans for larger procedures. All costs are quoted upfront with no hidden charges.

Travel & Logistics

How do I get a visa for medical treatment in India?+

Most nationalities can apply for India's e-Medical Visa online via the official Indian government visa portal. Fees and processing times vary by nationality; many approvals complete within a few business days. An attendant visa is often available for one companion. We provide the hospital invitation letter and guide you through the steps for your passport.

How long is the journey to India?+

Flight time depends on your origin. From Europe or the Middle East, direct or one-stop routes to Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, or Bangalore often take roughly 7–14 hours. From the Americas, expect longer hauls or connections via Europe or the Gulf. From Asia-Pacific hubs, many cities have direct services under 6–10 hours. We help you plan arrival timing around your hospital schedule.

How long will I need to stay in India?+

Typical stay durations: Hair transplant — 3-5 days. Dental implants/veneers — 5-7 days. Knee/hip replacement — 10-14 days. Cardiac surgery — 14-21 days. IVF — 15-20 days per cycle. LASIK — 2-3 days. We recommend adding a few extra recovery days, which many patients spend at wellness retreats in Kerala, Goa, or Rishikesh.

Where will I stay during treatment?+

Options include hospital-affiliated guest houses, partner four- and five-star hotels with medical shuttle service, serviced apartments near the hospital, and post-treatment recovery retreats. We arrange options based on your budget and preferences, and all accommodation is vetted for proximity to your hospital.

Can a family member accompany me?+

Absolutely — and we encourage it. India offers a Medical Visa for Attendant (e-MV-A) that allows one companion. Many of our partner hospitals have family-friendly facilities including visitor lounges, cafeterias, and nearby accommodation. We can arrange for both patient and companion logistics as a package.

Specific Treatments

Why is India so popular for hair transplants?+

India offers a strong price-to-quality ratio for hair transplants. A 3000-graft FUE procedure is typically a fraction of the cost in high-income markets. Indian trichologists perform very high volumes (many do 300+ procedures per year), which supports refined technique and natural results. English-speaking staff and comfortable recovery settings are standard at partner clinics.

Are dental materials in India the same quality as in Western clinics?+

Yes — our partner dental clinics use premium international materials: Nobel Biocare, Straumann, and BioHorizons implants; Ivoclar E-max and zirconia ceramics for crowns and veneers. These are the same brands used in leading clinics worldwide. You can request specific brands and verify materials used.

How does the free consultation work?+

When you book, our medical advisor contacts you within 24 hours via phone, video call, or WhatsApp. They review your medical history, discuss treatment options, recommend hospitals and doctors, and provide a detailed cost estimate in your preferred currency. There is absolutely no obligation to proceed. Many patients have two or three calls before making a decision.
