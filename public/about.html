About MedRouteIndia

Hassle-Free, Affordable
World-Class Treatment for All

We believe no one should have to choose between quality healthcare and financial security. MedRouteIndia bridges the gap between world-class Indian hospitals and patients across the globe who deserve better care at fair prices.

Our Mission

MedRouteIndia was born from a simple but powerful idea: the best medical care in the world shouldn't be reserved for the few who can afford it. India's JCI-accredited hospitals deliver outcomes that match — and often exceed — the best in the US, UK, and Europe. Yet treatment costs are 60-90% lower.

We exist to make this accessible to everyone. No middlemen inflating prices. No confusing paperwork. No navigating a foreign healthcare system alone. Just a single, trusted partner who handles everything — from your first enquiry to your safe return home.

"Every patient deserves a dedicated team, a clear plan, and zero surprises — regardless of their budget."

That's not a slogan. It's a promise we keep for every one of our 10,000+ patients.

What We Stand For

Six principles that guide every decision we make — from choosing partner hospitals to designing your treatment plan.

💰

Radical Affordability

We negotiate directly with hospitals to pass the lowest possible prices to you. No hidden fees, no surprise charges. Your quote is your final price.

💪

Zero Compromise on Quality

We only partner with JCI or NABH-accredited hospitals. Your surgeon has the same credentials and equipment as the best in New York or London.

🤝

Visa, flights, accommodation, airport pickup, hospital appointments, language support, post-op care — we handle every single detail so you can focus on healing.

No fine print. We share doctor credentials, hospital accreditations, real patient reviews, and itemised cost breakdowns before you commit to anything.

🌎

For Everyone, Everywhere

Our Story

From a small team with a big idea to India's most trusted medical tourism platform.

2019 — The Beginning

Founded with a Mission

MedRouteIndia started when our founders saw first-hand how international patients struggled to navigate India's healthcare system — despite it being world-class. We set out to build the bridge.

2020 — Growth

1,000 Patients, 20 Countries

Within a year, word of mouth and genuine results brought patients from the Middle East, UK, Australia, and Africa. We partnered with Apollo, Fortis, and Medanta.

2022 — Expansion

Full-Service Concierge Launched

We added visa support, airport meet-and-greet, recovery holiday planning, and 24/7 WhatsApp coordination. The entire journey became truly hassle-free.

2024 — AI Integration

AI-Powered Treatment Planning

We launched our AI chat agent — powered by advanced language models — that gives patients instant, personalised treatment plans and downloadable PDFs within seconds.

2026 — Today

10,000+ Patients, 50+ Countries

Today, MedRouteIndia serves patients from over 50 countries, partners with 44 JCI-accredited hospitals, and has saved patients over $120 million in treatment costs.

Every patient — regardless of treatment, budget, or country — receives the same standard of care, support, and dedication from our team.
