Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to get medical treatment in India?+

Absolutely. India has 44 JCI-accredited hospitals — the same accreditation body that certifies top US and European hospitals. Our partner hospitals maintain infection rates, surgical outcomes, and patient safety metrics comparable to the best worldwide. Many Indian surgeons trained at institutions like Mayo Clinic, Johns Hopkins, Cleveland Clinic, and Oxford.

How much can I actually save?+

Savings typically range from 60-90% depending on the procedure. A hair transplant that costs $15,000-20,000 in Western countries is $1,200-2,500 in India. Dental implants at $5,000+ per tooth at home cost $500 in India. Even including flights and accommodation, you'll save significantly. Use our cost calculator for specific estimates.

Does Medicare or my private health insurance cover treatment in India?+

Most national health systems and standard insurance plans do not cover overseas treatment. However, some private health insurers may partially reimburse certain procedures — check your policy. We recommend purchasing comprehensive travel medical insurance that covers medical tourism. Many patients find that even with insurance gaps, the savings in India far exceed the total out-of-pocket cost. Medical expenses may also be tax-deductible in some countries — consult your accountant.

What if something goes wrong after I return home?+

All our patients receive telemedicine follow-up access with their Indian specialist for 12 months post-treatment (lifetime for Royal Package). If physical intervention is needed, we coordinate with doctors in your home country who work with our network. Our partner hospitals also have international patient guarantee policies covering complications within the warranty period.

How long do I need to stay in India?+

It depends on the procedure: Hair transplant — 3-5 days. Dental implants/veneers — 5-7 days. Knee/hip replacement — 10-14 days. Cardiac surgery — 14-21 days. IVF — 15-20 days per cycle. We recommend adding a few recovery days, which many patients spend at wellness retreats in Kerala or Goa.

How does the free consultation work?+

When you book a free consultation, our medical advisor contacts you within 24 hours via phone, video call, or WhatsApp — whatever suits you. They'll review your medical history, discuss treatment options, recommend hospitals and doctors, provide a detailed cost estimate in USD (or your preferred currency), and answer all your questions. There's no obligation to proceed.

What about the visa process?+

Citizens of most countries can apply for India's e-Medical Visa online. It's typically approved within 72 hours, costs approximately $25-50, and is valid for 60 days with triple entry. You can also get an attendant visa for a family member. We provide the hospital invitation letter and assist with the entire visa process for your country.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to travel to India for surgery?

Yes. India’s JCI-accredited hospitals follow the same clinical protocols as top US and European hospitals. Medical tourism is a government-supported industry with dedicated visa categories and patient protection regulations.

How long do I need to stay in India for treatment?

It varies by procedure: hair transplant 3–5 days, dental implants 5–7 days, knee replacement 10–14 days, IVF 15–20 days per cycle. We plan your stay precisely so you know before booking flights.

Do Indian doctors speak English?

All our partner doctors are fluent in English. Medical education in India is conducted entirely in English. Many of our surgeons trained in the US, UK, or Australia.

Will my insurance cover treatment in India?

Some international health insurance policies cover overseas treatment. We recommend checking with your insurer first. Regardless, the out-of-pocket cost in India is typically less than the deductible alone in many Western countries.

What if I need follow-up care after returning home?

Every MedRouteIndia package includes 12 months of telemedicine follow-up with your surgeon. For physical follow-ups, we can coordinate with partner clinics in your home country or arrange return visits.
