Common Questions

What exactly happens when I pay $150?
Our on-ground team takes your medical files to the doctor of your choice, studies your case with them in person, connects you on a live video call with the doctor, understands the full procedure and whether treatment is required, gets the doctor's earliest available dates, and curates a complete arrival-to-departure plan — all shared with you within 5-7 business days.
Can I choose ANY doctor in India?
Yes. You can name a specific doctor or hospital, or ask us to recommend the best options based on your condition and budget. We visit whoever you choose in our 6 operating cities (Delhi, Jaipur, Mumbai, Bangalore, Kolkata, Chennai).
Is the $150 refundable if I decide not to proceed?
The $150 covers the cost of our on-ground team visiting the doctor, studying your case, arranging a video call, and preparing your complete plan. It is non-refundable. However, if the doctor is unavailable or we cannot complete the visit for any reason, you receive a full refund.
What happens after I receive the report?
You review the doctor's opinion, treatment plan, and cost breakdown. If you decide to proceed, you pay the 1st installment (enrollment fee) which covers visa assistance, hotel booking, flight coordination, and care coordinator assignment. The final payment is made on or before treatment day.
How long does the process take?
Once you pay the $150, we schedule the doctor visit within 3-5 business days. After the visit and video call, the complete plan (procedure details, dates, costs, travel timeline) is shared with you within 5-7 business days. Follow-up calls are scheduled at your convenience within the next 2 weeks.
What is included in the 1st installment?
The 1st installment is the service enrollment fee, payable once you decide to proceed. It covers: visa assistance, hotel reservation, flight coordination support, care coordinator assignment, pre-treatment telemedicine call with the doctor, and all logistical planning. The exact amount depends on your treatment and chosen services.
When do I pay the final amount?
The final payment — covering the treatment, hospital fees, and any remaining service charges — is paid on or before the day your treatment begins at the hospital. You can pay via bank transfer, credit card, or at the hospital directly.
